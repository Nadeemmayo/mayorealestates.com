How overseas Pakistanis can buy property safely

Video tours, power of attorney and paying through proper banking channels — buying from abroad, step by step.

By Wakeel Mayo · 12 September 2026

Thousands of overseas Pakistanis buy a home back home every year. Here is how to do it safely while you are abroad.

Step by step

  1. Shortlist with video: we send live video tours and real photos of each property.
  2. Verify: we check the documents and share copies with you.
  3. Power of attorney: appoint a trusted family member through a registered special power of attorney, attested by the Pakistani embassy or consulate where you live.
  4. Pay through banks: send funds through proper banking channels to keep a clear record.
  5. Transfer: your attorney completes the transfer; we attend every step.

Many Pakistani banks also offer accounts and home finance designed for non-resident Pakistanis. Ask us for an introduction.
